INTERNAL AI CONCEPT

Internal Data Intelligence Assistant

A private, governed AI experience that allows stakeholders to ask questions, understand Tableau dashboards, query approved Databricks data, generate visual insights and escalate unresolved questions to the correct human team.

THE IDEA

What if stakeholders could simply ask the data?

Today, stakeholders often depend on Data and Analytics teams to understand dashboards, interpret KPIs, retrieve information or investigate changes. This project explores a secure AI-powered experience where stakeholders can ask those questions directly using natural language and receive trusted answers from approved enterprise data.
